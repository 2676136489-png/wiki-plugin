Wiki Plugins

A set of copyable interaction blocks for modern wiki pages: light motion, clear evidence, compact navigation, and responsive static-first design.

Float

悬浮工具坞

适合长页面快速跳转、返回顶部、打开搜索。

 Motion

磁吸卡片

 Text

 Media
